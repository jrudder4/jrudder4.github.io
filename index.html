About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Applied Economics Department at Oregon State University. I have a PhD in Agricultural and Resource Economics from the University of California, Davis, and was a postdoctoral scholar at the Development Innovation Lab at the University of Chicago.

My research interests are development economics, industrial organization, agriculture, and applied econometrics.
